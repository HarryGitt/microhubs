# Specific Target 1: Analysis of HOLM Context, Urban Logistics Environment, and Data Landscape
The first target is to develop a structured overview of HOLM’s role within
the EU Horizon project and the broader urban logistics environment. 
Students will analyze HOLM’s mission, stakeholder network, and innovation
focus. They will examine the logistics challenges in urban areas, 
including congestion, delivery density, accessibility constraints, 
and sustainability goals.
Students will identify relevant datasets such as delivery demand patterns,
trafficflows, land-use maps, public transport networks, cycling infrastructure,
demographic indicators, and zoning constraints. This analysis will form the
foundation for the micro-depot location model.


# Specific Target 2: Development of a Micro-Depot Location Model
The second target is to develop a data-driven model for identifying optimal micro-depot locations. 
Students will analyze geospatial data to identify
hotspots of delivery demand, accessibility corridors, and areas with suit
able infrastructure. The model will consider factors such as proximity to
delivery clusters, cycling network connectivity, land availability, regulatory
constraints, and environmental impact.
The analysis will include geospatial clustering, heatmap generation,
accessibility scoring, and scenario comparison. Students will propose a structured
decision framework that HOLM can use to evaluate potential micro-depot
sites. The nal deliverable will include geospatial visualizations, a location
scoring matrix, and recommendations for pilot deployment.
