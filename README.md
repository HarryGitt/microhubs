# Specific Target 1: Analysis of HOLM Context, Urban Logistics Environment, and Data Landscape
The first target is to develop a structured overview of HOLM’s role within
the EU Horizon project and the broader urban logistics environment. Stu
dents will analyze HOLM’s mission, stakeholder network, and innovation
focus. They will examine the logistics challenges in urban areas, includ
ing congestion, delivery density, accessibility constraints, and sustainability
goals.
Students will identify relevant datasets such as delivery demand patterns,
tra c ows, land-use maps, public transport networks, cycling infrastructure,
demographic indicators, and zoning constraints. This analysis will form the
foundation for the micro-depot location model.
